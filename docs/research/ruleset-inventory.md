# Ruleset inventory

Which instructionals in the corpus are gi, no-gi, or mixed. Primary source: the instructional folder name, then speech in that instructional's own volumes. A volume is one JSON file. Nothing outside the corpus was used.

Corpus root: `C:\Users\dumbo\Documents\GitHub\djsciman_data_projects\video-transcriber\library`

A folder name that already says gi or no-gi is the citation. Otherwise the label comes from speech. Lapel, pants, sleeve, collar of the jacket, belt, or skirt supports gi. Explicit no-gi wording, or instruction that refuses the kimono, supports no-gi. Both are marked mixed only when both are taught as the subject of the series. A parenthetical ("this also works in the other ruleset", "if we were training without a gi") does not make the series mixed. If the speech never settles it, the label is not decidable.

## Decision

The ruleset is not known for the whole corpus. It is known for 26 of the 32 instructionals: 16 gi, 8 no-gi, and 2 mixed. The two mixed series cannot take one ruleset on every citation, so the coach still has to label those chapters one by one. The coach must also label the four instructionals whose speech never settles a ruleset, and the two empty instructionals that have a folder and no volumes.
